DEM source:
1. Official DEM source
The appropriate product is LOLA LDEM_256, where:
- LOLA = Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter
- LDEM = LOLA Digital Elevation Model
- Resolution: 256 pixels/degree
- Pixel spacing: 118.4505876 m at the equator
- Projection: Equirectangular / Simple Cylindrical
- Latitude convention: planetocentric
- Longitude: positive east
- Data type: 16-bit
- Coverage: global, divided into four tiles by longitude and hemisphere.
